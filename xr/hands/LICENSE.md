# Hand models

`left.glb` / `right.glb` are the `generic-hand` models from the
[WebXR Input Profiles](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles) assets package
(`@webxr-input-profiles/assets@1.0.20`, `dist/profiles/generic-hand/`), used unmodified.
Their 25 skin joints are named after the WebXR hand joints and follow the WebXR joint axis
convention (-Z along the bone toward the fingertip, +Y out of the back of the hand).
